# Lightmap carving redesign (#52)

Results and decisions live on issue #52. This file holds the method and the delegation log.

## Step 0: measuring the ceiling

`game/bin/x64/carve_ceiling.py EVIDENCE.bsp --cost OPTIMIZED.bsp [--json out.json]` (method in its docstring).

- EVIDENCE: the lit baseline lmoptimizer caches in `<map dir>/.lmopt_cache/`.
- OPTIMIZED: the lmoptimizer output VMF compiled with `vbsp_lmo -emitsideids -game <dir>`. VBSP only, no VIS/RAD needed.
- Envelope check: set every `"lightmapscale"` in the optimized VMF to 64 and run `vbsp_lmo -countverts -game <dir>`. The difference from the optimized count is the most any scale change can remove.
- Maps used on 2026-11-07, `-game` = `Half-Life 2 Deathmatch/hl2mp` (GarrysMod has no bin/x64; lmoptimizer refuses it): `unit_test_maps/d1_trainstation_02_hrcs.vmf` (cached baseline) and `bsp_unit_tests/fightspace3_unittest.vmf` (no cached baseline; an uncached compile hits #53, worked around with short dotless scratch names).
- Runtime is about 45 s on trainstation and a few minutes on fightspace3, all in Python.

## Step 2: per-side evidence scale (opt-in)

The plain solver raises a whole side only when its baseline lightmap is exactly flat AND dark. Step 0 showed many lit, smooth sides reproduced at a coarser scale. `--smooth-tolerance` lets the solver use that.

- `game/bin/x64/lightmap_evidence.py`: `coarsest_reproducible_scale(ld, tolerance)` takes a `bsp_reader.FaceLightmapData` and returns b*k for the largest factor k in (2, 4, 8, 16, 32) at which EVERY luxel of EVERY sampled sub-face is within `tolerance` (display space, out of 255) of the bilinear reconstruction from every k-th luxel, on both the aligned grid and the grid shifted by half a step (`worst_recon_error`; on the shifted grid a coarse luxel just off each edge reads the edge luxel, as VRAD samples an off-face luxel at the nearest point on the face, so slopes reaching an edge are flattened by about slope*k/4). Sub-faces fewer than `MIN_SAMPLED_LUXELS` (3) luxels across are skipped: no luxel centre lies on them, so their lightmap is extrapolation. b is the finest (minimum) sub-face scale, narrow ones included: each sampled sub-face is tested at k on its own luxel grid, so b*k is the only scale validated for all of them. 0 means no evidence: nothing passes, never-visible, no sub-faces, only too-narrow sub-faces, or a sub-face with missing/wrong-length luxels or no `lightmap_vecs`. `evidence_scales(face_data, tolerance)` maps it over a dict and keeps the non-zero ones. `display` and `recon_error` (public) moved here from `carve_ceiling.py`, which imports them (`carve_ceiling` still uses the aligned grid only, so its step 0 numbers are unchanged).
- `lmoptimizer.py --smooth-tolerance [T]` (default off; bare flag = 1.0, strict; 4.0 is loose; 0 or negative = off with a warning; the GUI field "Smooth tol." sets it, 0 = off): `_smooth_evidence` computes the dict from `face_data` just before `solve_with_vbsp(evidence_scales=...)`, leaving out the sides the visibility check marked never-visible (`_mark_never_visible` only flattens their luminances; their sub-faces keep real lighting), and prints one summary line. That line counts candidates, before capping, grouping and the baseline comparison. lmoptimizer passes the coplanar groups as `evidence_groups` in both coplanar modes; `coplanar_groups` (atomic promotion) stays None under `--strict-coplanar`. The calibration fallback (`budget_solver.py`) ignores the flag and warns.
- `vbsp_solver.solve_with_vbsp(evidence_scales=, evidence_groups=)`: only entries that are not uniform or emissive (eligible) take evidence. Over each group (`evidence_groups`, else `coplanar_groups`) the eligible members share the minimum of their RAW evidence; ineligible members have no say, and an eligible member with no evidence (e.g. a carve-created side) gives the group 0. Each member then caps that at its own `max_useful_scale`; a capped value <= 1 is no floor. The floor applies as `max(scale, min_scale, evidence_scale)` in the Phase 1 scan and the scale=1 early exit. After Phase 1 an entry whose floor is >= `max(baseline scale, min_scale)` stays at `max(baseline, min_scale, floor)` and is not a promotion candidate; it is FLOORED (counted as raised) only when the floor is strictly above, and an equal floor counts as at baseline. Others stay promotable; a promoted one goes to `max(1, min_scale, evidence_scale)` (the evidence says finer is wasted), in the Phase 2 trials and the final assignment alike.
- `VBSPSolverResult.faces_evidence_raised`: the number of floored entries (strictly above `max(baseline, min_scale)`; an equal floor counts in `faces_at_baseline`, in the Phase 2 result and the scale-1 early exit alike). Shown in the results box as "Evidence raised" whenever the flag is set.
- Test: `unit_test/lmoptimizer/test_smooth_evidence.py` (the reconstruction rule on synthetic sub-faces; the solver floor with `count_vertices` mocked, including vacuous floors that must not block promotion, a floor below the baseline, strict-mode groups and the Phase 2 trial counts; the never-visible exclusion through `_mark_never_visible` and `_smooth_evidence`).

## Measuring quality: the finer reference

`lightmap_verify.py BASE NEW` (raw difference) is not a quality measure. VRAD's fast flags (`-noextra`) point-sample each luxel, so ANY change of luxel grid moves every shadow edge: on fightspace3 the sides lmoptimizer made finer (12 -> 11) differ from the baseline more than the evidence-raised ones (43% of them over p99 4). The step 2 recommendation to "accept when no raised side exceeds p99 4" could not be met even by going finer.

Instead:
1. `lit_compile.py MAP.vmf --game DIR --out DIR --name REF --scale 6 --maxlightmapdim 64 --rtx [--texture-shadows LEVEL] [--vbsp/--vvis/--vrad]` builds a uniformly finer reference with the baseline's own pipeline (vbsp_lmo -emitsideids, vvis -fast, VRAD fast flags; the map's `<map>.rad` is copied under the output stem, `--lights`/`--binroot` pass through; `--texture-shadows none|props|world|translucent|all` (default props = `-textureshadows`) must equal the level lmoptimizer's baseline used, and both should match the final compile's flag; lmoptimizer's texlight light-budget fit is not reproduced). `--maxlightmapdim 64` at scale 6 keeps the scale-12 face splits, so it fits the 65,536-vertex limit; displacement sides keep their own scale (VRAD caps them at 125 luxels; verify skips them anyway). Use this repo's `vrad_rtx.exe`: the older `E:/GitHub/vrad-rtx` build overflows a fixed 35x35 sample buffer on faces over 32 luxels (heap corruption, 0xC0000374).
2. `lightmap_verify.py BASE NEW --reference REF.bsp` samples BASE and NEW at the reference's luxels and reports each side's loss = p99|NEW-REF| - p99|BASE-REF|, split into raised / control / finer classes. The finer class is the noise floor of any scale change; judge the raised class against it, not against zero.
3. A side's loss also moves when its lighting changes for other reasons (bounce, light settings): fightspace3 side 90924 lost 34 both when raised and when made finer. Judge rates against the noise floor, not single sides.

## Bounce light

VRAD sizes bounce patches in luxels (`maxchop`/`minchop`, default 4, `src/utils/vrad/vrad.cpp`), so a side's scale also sets the patch size it emits and receives bounce with. lmoptimizer reads one baseline (2 bounces, `vrad_runner.FAST_VRAD_ARGS`) and never re-lights, so a scale change's effect on OTHER sides' bounce is invisible to it.

Method (`game/bin/x64/bounce_attribution.py`, docstring has the usage): compile BASE and NEW with `lit_compile.py --bounce 2` and `--bounce 0`. On "control" sides (scale unchanged) the luxel lattice is identical, so their direct light must match (the 0-bounce pair checks it) and any 2-bounce difference is other sides' bounce. Caveat: VRAD skips patch subdivision at `-bounce 0` (`SubdividePatches`), so texture lights are emitted from one patch per face there; on maps with surface lights the 0-bounce build is not a pure direct-light twin and `bounce_share` is approximate (trainstation has none: 36 lights in both builds). The control-side figures compare 2-bounce builds and do not depend on it. `--noise` takes a repeat BASE build. `--emitters coarser|finer|changed` picks which scale-changed sides count toward a control side's exposure (power = mean linear luminance x area x reflectivity, over the squared gap between the two sides' bounding boxes clamped to at least 16 units, facing pairs only, within `--radius`; overlapping boxes count as 16 units apart, so it ranks, it does not model transfer). To split cause, `split_vmf`-style variants of BASE carrying only NEW's scale increases or only its decreases (done in scratch: match sides by id, rewrite `lightmapscale`).

Results, trainstation, 2026-11-08 (lmoptimizer at master f5e829a2, `--smooth-tolerance`, cached baseline; `-rtx`, hl2mp):
- Noise: a repeat BASE build differs on 4 of 3,127 sides by more than 4/255 (one side at 24, the same side in every comparison). Direct light on control sides is identical except 44 sides whose faces changed for other reasons.
- Coarsening only (3,700 sides raised, 322 measurable): 17 of 2,805 control sides over 4, median 0.13. About noise. Coarsening bright emitters barely moves neighbours' bounce.
- Refining only (717 sides, mostly 2 -> 1): 255 of 2,530 control sides over 4, 140 over 8, max 57; 156 brighter, 99 darker; local (high p99, small mean shift). Exposure to refined sides ranks with it (Spearman 0.40, quartile medians 0.35 / 0.67 / 0.89 / 1.47; 244 of the 255 are near a refined side). The full lmoptimizer output matches this (260 over 4; with `--emitters changed`, Spearman 0.43 and 251 of the 260 near a changed side; with `--emitters coarser` only 0.23, 144 of 260).
- Not a GPU/CPU artefact: the refined maps' transfers (1.2 G -> 3.8 G, 9 -> 29 GB of transfer data, 48 GB commit) do not fit in VRAM, which turns GPU bounces off (#84: the old INT_MAX guard was stale; the limit is free VRAM, about 2.1 G transfers on the 24 GB RTX 3090 with the OptiX scene loaded). VRAD `-nogpubounce` (added for this; `lit_compile.py --vrad-arg=-nogpubounce`) on BASE matches the GPU build to noise.
- Which of the two bounce solutions is closer to the truth is not measured: a finer reference cannot be built within the transfer budget. The coarser neighbour of a refined side is not wrong, only different.

Consequences: bounce gives no reason to keep a side fine (its bounce detail is band-limited by its 4-luxel patches, and coarsening an emitter is harmless). Promotion to scale 1 has a cost the solver does not see: VRAD transfer memory roughly triples on trainstation (#83; past about 2.1 G transfers on the 24 GB RTX 3090 the bounces leave the GPU, #84). The baseline's bounce on sides next to promoted ones is stale, which also justifies the dark-only rule for flat-side pre-promotion (`vbsp_solver.py`).

`lightmap_verify._side_errors` skips b faces whose padded box misses an a face's luxels (identical output on 422 sides, 11x faster; a 223-face side took 33 s) and takes `signed=True`.

## --chop and never-visible sides (#79, #86)

- Texlight sides are never carve candidates (`find_carve_candidates(exclude_sides=)`): the solvers never promote them, so a carve would only add brushes.
- With `--visibility-check`, a BSP face bspwalk reports never visible is promotable in the carver like a dark sub-face (`hidden_faces=`). A side that a visible sliver keeps out of the whole-side override (`_mark_never_visible` needs every BSP face hidden) gets its hidden part carved off and maxed; the pieces over visible faces keep their scale. Chosen over area weighting (max the side when the visible faces are under X% of its area) because that coarsens faces a player sees, against the false-never-visible rule. Limits: only `--chop`, only axis-aligned brushes without displacements, and the piece is inset one luxel like dark carves. Without `--chop` the run prints how many sides are partly never-visible. Whether the 2 visible faces of trainstation side 116803 are real is still open (#85, `-whyface`).
